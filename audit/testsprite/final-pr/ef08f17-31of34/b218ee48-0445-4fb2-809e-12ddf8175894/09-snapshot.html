02How it counts

Instant runoff, one round at a time.

  1. 01Read every ballot

    Cast vote records become ranked patterns. Overvotes, skipped ranks and repeats are kept, not cleaned away.

  2. 02Count first choices

    Each ballot counts once, for its highest-ranked candidate who is still in the race.

  3. 03Eliminate and transfer

    The last-place candidate is out. Their ballots move to the next valid ranking, or become inactive.

  4. 04Reach a majority

    Rounds repeat until one candidate holds more than half of the ballots still continuing.

04Open API

One request. The whole count.

Send ballots as count,A>B>C lines. Use _ for a skipped rank and [A=B] for an overvote. Add seats for STV. Every round, transfer and inactive category comes back as JSON.
